# 🛡️ Razorpay Payment Recovery & Guardrail Policy Standard
**Document Code:** `RZP-POL-REC-2026.08`  
**Target Platform:** Razorpay Payment Gateway, Razorpay Optimizer & ReviveAI Autonomous Recovery Agent  
**Compliance Standard:** RBI e-Mandate Circular 2021, NPCI UPI Procedural Guidelines 2024, PCI-DSS v4.0  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

In Indian digital payments, payment failure rates range between **15% to 35%** across recurring SaaS subscriptions, D2C checkout, and B2B invoices. This document formalizes the **algorithmic recovery, retry guardrails, and customer communication policies** for ReviveAI integrated with **Razorpay's Payment Stack** (Razorpay PG, Razorpay Optimizer, Turbo UPI, and Thirdwatch Risk Engine).

The primary objective is **maximizing recovered Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)** while strictly preventing:
1. Card network/bank rate-limiting and penalty charges.
2. Involuntary customer churn due to aggressive retry spam.
3. Fraud exposure on compromised cards or spoofed VPAs.
4. Non-compliance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and NPCI recurring mandate frameworks.

---

## 2. Razorpay Payment Failure Taxonomy & Classification

Every declined transaction captured by Razorpay webhook `payment.failed` is ingested and mapped into one of four core categories:

| Error Category | Razorpay Error Code / Reason | Recovery Rail | Failure Nature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transient Technical** | `GATEWAY_ERROR`, `NETWORK_ERROR`, `SERVER_ERROR`, `AUTHENTICATION_FAILURE` | **Auto-Retry / Smart Routing** | Temporary (Gateway downtime, 3DS timeout, SMS OTP latency) |
| **Payer Balance / Limit** | `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `EXCEED_DAILY_LIMIT` | **Smart Cooldown + In-App Push** | Actionable / Cyclical (Salary credit cycles, bank limit reset) |
| **Instrument Invalidation** | `CARD_EXPIRED`, `INVALID_CARD`, `INVALID_CVV`, `ACCOUNT_BLOCKED` | **Customer Update Link (Magic Portal)** | Permanent on current instrument |
| **Risk / Fraud Flag** | `FRAUD_RISK_ERROR`, `SUSPICIOUS_VELOCITY`, `THIRDWATCH_FLAG` | **Strict Manual Review Escalation** | High Risk (Stolen credentials, high chargeback score) |

## 3. The 6 Golden Guardrail Policies

All AI and ML recommendations generated by the Recovery Agent **MUST** pass through the **Policy Engine** before invoking any action tool.

```

│   + LLM AI Diagnosis    │

            │
            ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐

└───────────┬─────────────┘
      ┌─────┴─────┐
      │           │
      ▼           ▼
  [APPROVED]   [BLOCKED] ──► Fallback Action (Escalate / Payment Link)
      │

```

### 🔴 Rule 1: Maximum Retry Ceiling (Anti-Fatigue Guardrail)
- **Policy Code:** `POL-01-MAX-RETRY`
- **Rule:** A single transaction invoice shall **NEVER** exceed **5 total retry attempts** across its entire lifecycle.
- **Rationale:** Prevents excessive merchant authorization fees charged by acquiring banks and avoids cardholder harassment.
- **Enforcement:** If `retry_count >= 5` and Agent recommends `retry`, the Policy Engine **blocks** the retry and automatically overrides the decision to `escalate` (route to Customer Success).

---

### 🔴 Rule 2: Fraud & Risk Isolation (Razorpay Thirdwatch Guardrail)
- **Policy Code:** `POL-02-FRAUD-ISOLATION`
- **Rule:** Any transaction tagged with `failure_reason == "fraud_flag"` or Razorpay Thirdwatch risk score $\ge 70$ is **PERMANENTLY BLOCKED** from autonomous retries.
- **Rationale:** Protects merchants against chargebacks, card brand dispute programs, and illegal authorization attempts.
- **Enforcement:** Mandatory Level-3 human review escalation.

---

### 🔴 Rule 3: High-Value Revenue Protection Threshold
- **Policy Code:** `POL-03-HIGH-VALUE-PROTECT`
- **Rule:** For high-value transactions (`amount > ₹25,000`), if the ML model predicts recovery probability $P(\text{recovery}) < 30\%$, autonomous silent retries are **FORBIDDEN**.
- **Rationale:** High-ticket B2B and Enterprise accounts require VIP white-glove communication rather than automated blind retries.
- **Enforcement:** Escalation created with priority `urgent` assigned to dedicated Key Account Managers.

---

### 🟡 Rule 4: Stale Failure Window & Invoice Expiry
- **Policy Code:** `POL-04-STALE-WINDOW`
- **Rule:** Transactions where `time_since_failure_hours > 168` (7 days) shall **NOT** be silently retried.
- **Rationale:** Cardholder account state and authorization tokens degrade over time; attempting silent charges on week-old transactions creates high dispute rates.
- **Enforcement:** Action modified to `notify_customer` with a dynamic Razorpay Payment Link dispatched via WhatsApp/Email.

---

### 🟡 Rule 5: Mandated Customer Notification Trigger
- **Policy Code:** `POL-05-CUSTOMER-NOTIFY`
- **Rule:** When a transaction reaches `retry_count >= 2`, customer notification is **MANDATORY** in tandem with or preceding any subsequent retry.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the user informed and encourages balance top-up or alternate payment method usage.

---

### 🟢 Rule 6: NPCI & Bank Cooldown Window
- **Policy Code:** `POL-06-RETRY-COOLDOWN`
- **Rule:** Retries must observe a minimum cooldown of **2 hours** (for gateway timeouts) and **24 hours** (for insufficient funds).
- **Rationale:** Immediate back-to-back retries on bank downtime or empty balance fail with $>95\%$ certainty, wasting gateway quota.

## 4. Multi-Rail Failover & Razorpay Optimizer Integration

When primary payment instruments fail persistently, the agent initiates smart instrument switching:

1. **Card Failure (Expired/3DS Declined) $\to$ Razorpay Magic Update Link**:
   - Generates a tokenized hosted link (`https://pay.reviveai.io/update/{customer_id}`) allowing card re-entry with RBI-compliant tokenization.
2. **Bank Downtime $\to$ Alternative Rail Offer**:
   - Seamlessly switches billing route to **UPI AutoPay** or **Netbanking (Direct Bank API)**.

## 5. Audit Trail, Explainability & Compliance

In adherence to financial governance, **100% of agent decisions are immutably logged** in the database (`recovery_decisions` table):
- `audit_id`: Unique trace ID in format `REC-{txn_suffix}-{timestamp}`.
- `ml_recovery_probability`: Exact calibrated statistical probability $P(\text{recovery})$.
- `llm_diagnosis` & `llm_reasoning`: Explainable AI diagnosis of root cause.
- `policy_check_passed` & `policy_violations`: Full checklist of policies evaluated.
- `final_action`: Executed action approved by policy guardrails.
